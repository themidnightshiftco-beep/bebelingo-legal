Frequently asked questions

What is the parental gate?

Settings, purchases and external links sit behind a parental gate so young children do not open them by accident. Tap the correct answer to a two-digit addition question shown in words and digits. The gate stays open for a few minutes.

Where are the settings?

Tap the small gear icon in the top-right corner of the home screen and pass the parental gate. Child profile, sound, speech recognition, time limit, subscription and privacy are there.

How do I restore my purchase?

Parent area → Subscription → Restore purchases, signed in with the Apple account used to subscribe.

How do I cancel or get a refund?

iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Bebelingo → Cancel Subscription. Cancelling during the trial means no charge. Refunds are requested through Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.

There is no sound.

Turn up the phone volume and check Parent area → Sound and speech → volume. The “Sound test” there plays Bebo and an animal sound. Animal and vehicle sounds can be switched on or off on the same screen.

Speech mode does not turn on.

Speech mode works only on devices that support on-device Turkish speech recognition and needs microphone and speech recognition permission (iPhone Settings → Bebelingo). With speech mode off, every game is played by tapping.

How do I change the daily time limit?

Parent area → Time limit and sleep. When time is up Bebo goes to sleep; a parent can allow a little more play for that day.

How do I delete our data?

Parent area → Delete all data and reset. After confirmation, the child’s name, progress, stars, settings and scheduled reminders are removed from the device. Deleting the app also removes all data. The subscription stays with your Apple account. Details: Privacy Policy.
