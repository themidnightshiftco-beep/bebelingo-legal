Privacy Policy

Effective date: 24 October 2026 · Türkçe sürüm (the Turkish version prevails)

In short: Bebelingo has no accounts, ads, analytics, crash reporting or tracking. Information about your child (name, age, progress) stays on your device. The app connects only to Apple and RevenueCat to buy and verify the subscription; an anonymous ID, purchase data and technical data such as the device’s identifier for vendor (IDFV) are processed for that; the advertising identifier (IDFA) is not used (section 5).

1. Who we are

Bebelingo is made by TheMidnightShift (Düzce, Türkiye): Turkish word and speaking games for children aged 2-5. Contact: omeryavuzzz7@gmail.com.

2. What is stored, where and why

To make the games work, the app stores the following only on your device (in the app’s own storage). It is not sent to any server and we have no access to it.

InformationPurpose
Child’s name (optional, first name suggested), age band (2-5), learning goals, chosen avatar (built-in drawings; no photos)Bebo can say the child’s name; age-appropriate words
Game progress (word, time, heard/found), stars, stickersRewards and the weekly summary in the parent area
Daily usage time (last 30 days) and daily time limitEnforcing the parent’s time limit
Settings: Bebo’s voice, volume, animal and vehicle sounds, reminder preferenceRunning the app the way you chose
Subscription status (trial/active/end date)Unlocking premium content

If you use Apple device backups (iCloud or computer), Apple may include this data in your device backup. That is between you and Apple.

The app does not collect location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or the advertising identifier (IDFA). The technical data sent to RevenueCat for subscription verification is described in section 5 below.

3. Microphone and speech recognition

Bebelingo does not use the microphone or speech recognition and does not ask for these permissions. Every game is played by tapping; the app does not record, listen to or send audio. (The optional “speech mode” of an earlier development version has been removed from the app.)

4. Notifications

If a parent starts the free trial and opts in, a local reminder is scheduled on the device 2 days before the trial ends. Permission is requested behind the parental gate. No push notifications are used.

5. Purchases and third parties

  • Subscriptions and payments are handled by the Apple App Store. We never see your payment details (card etc.); Apple’s privacy policy applies.
  • RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc., USA) is used to process and verify purchases (preventing fraudulent purchases and unlocking Premium) and to show us aggregate subscription statistics (e.g. trial and renewal counts). It receives: a random anonymous user ID generated by the app (not your name, e-mail or Apple ID) and the App Store purchase data (product, purchase/renewal/expiry dates, trial status, receipt). For purchase validation the RevenueCat library also adds the device’s identifier for vendor (IDFV), the app version, iOS version, device model, store country, language and IP address to its requests. No advertising identifier (IDFA) is sent and App Tracking Transparency (ATT) is not used; no attribution data or custom user ID is sent; nothing about your child (name, age, progress) is sent to RevenueCat. This data is not used for advertising or tracking. RevenueCat privacy policy: revenuecat.com/privacy.
  • There are no ad networks, analytics, crash reporting, social or tracking tools. The app connects to the internet only to buy and verify the subscription (Apple, RevenueCat).
  • Bebo’s voices, the animal and vehicle sounds, the drawings and the app icon were generated once with AI tools during development and are built into the app. No information about your child is sent to these tools while the app is used.

6. Children’s privacy

Bebelingo is designed for children under 13 (ages 2-5) and is set up with a parent. We do not collect personal data from children; there are no ads, tracking, chat or social features. Settings, purchases, notification permission and external links (like this page) are behind the parental gate. Child screens contain no purchase offers or external links.

7. Retention and deletion

  • Data stays on your device until you delete it. Usage time is limited to the last 30 days and progress records to the last 1,000 entries.
  • To delete: Parent area → Delete all data and reset (after a confirmation screen, all Bebelingo data and scheduled reminders are deleted), or delete the app.
  • The subscription belongs to your Apple account; it comes back with “Restore purchases”. Manage it in iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

8. Your rights

Purchase records are kept by Apple and RevenueCat under their own policies for managing the subscription and legal obligations; you can ask us to delete records linked to the anonymous ID (you may need to send the anonymous ID shown in the app).

Under Article 11 of Türkiye’s KVKK (Law No. 6698), the EU GDPR and the principles of the US COPPA, you have rights to know whether personal data is processed, to request information, correction, deletion and to object. Bebelingo keeps no personal data on its own servers; you can view and delete the data on your device at any time as described above. For questions and requests, write to omeryavuzzz7@gmail.com.

9. Changes

If a new service is added to the app, we will update this policy and its effective date, and announce important changes on this page together with the app update.
